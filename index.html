    VLM-Assisted Prognostics — Turbofan RUL on NASA C-MAPSS

    • Vision-Language Models
    • Deep Learning
    • PHM

    Built a two-branch prognostic framework that pairs eight numerical remaining-useful-life (RUL) models (XGBoost, BiLSTM, TCN, MS-TCN, Transformer and more) with Qwen2.5-VL-7B, which reads full-history plots of all 21 engine sensors and reports degradation trend, regime, a qualitative life band and figure legibility — or abstains when a plot is unreadable. A deterministic, bounded fusion rule keeps the final RUL under numerical control.

    Evaluated on all four C-MAPSS subsets (707 test engines) across three random seeds. Visual evidence did not improve RUL accuracy, but it exposed a strong reliability signal: the VLM judged 13–20% of figures unreadable under a single operating condition versus 58–60% under six — pointing to VLMs as a qualitative screening and evidence-quality layer rather than a numerical predictor.

    Status: Submitted for publication — Aerospace Science and Technology (Elsevier).

    • Python
    • PyTorch
    • Qwen2.5-VL
    • XGBoost
    • Transformers
    • Predictive Maintenance

    Project Neptune — Pneumatic Cobot Workcell Safety Assessment

    • ISO 13849-1
    • Functional Safety
    • Risk Assessment

    Conducted an ISO 13849-1 safety assessment of a pneumatic collaborative robot workcell featuring a Category 3 dual-channel safety architecture. Calculated MTTFd, DCavg, and PFHd (2.47 × 10⁻⁷/h) to verify compliance with Performance Level d (PLd) and SIL 2 requirements.

    Determined a minimum safety distance of 218 mm with a total system response time of ≤100 ms per ISO 13855, and proposed downstream pressure monitoring and automated valve testing to support future PLe/SIL 3 compliance.

    • ISO 13849-1
    • ISO 13855
    • Functional Safety
    • Machine Safety
    • Industrial Automation

    4-DOF RRRP AOI Robotic Manipulator

    • SolidWorks
    • Simscape Multibody
    • Control Systems

    Designed and simulated a 4-DOF RRRP robotic manipulator for Automated Optical Inspection (AOI) using SolidWorks, MATLAB Simulink, and Simscape Multibody. Developed inverse kinematics and smooth 3D trajectory planning across 8 inspection waypoints using polynomial interpolation and homogeneous transformation matrices.

    Implemented a PD controller (Kp = Kd = 5) with gravity and Coriolis compensation for accurate, stable trajectory tracking — achieving collision-free motion with minimal tracking error throughout a 14-second simulation.

    • MATLAB
    • Simulink
    • Simscape Multibody
    • Inverse Kinematics
    • Trajectory Planning
